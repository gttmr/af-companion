# E2 (skill collision) — Artifact tree

## Retained evidence surface

The experiment created no scenario output or repository source change. Its retained
evidence is exactly the seven-file surface required by `tests/skills/README.md`:

```text
artifact-tree.txt
commands.log
environment.md
prompt.md
result-summary.md
selected-skills.md
validation.txt
```

Raw transcripts are intentionally not retained because they carried extensive
local/session paths. The bounded skill inventory is in `selected-skills.md`; the
commands and outcomes are in `commands.log`, `validation.txt`, and
`result-summary.md`.
